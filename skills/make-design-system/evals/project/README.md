# Fieldnote

A quiet log for birders. Record what you saw, where, and when — then get out of the way.

Two surfaces:

- `index.html` — the landing page: hero, three features, pricing, footer
- `app.html` — the app: a dense, filterable table of sightings

Audience: hobbyist birders, mostly on a phone in the field, sometimes on a laptop at
home tidying up the day's list. Brand colour is the green in `styles/tokens.css`.
