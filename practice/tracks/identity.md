# Track — race, power and people in tech (Wed)

A competence track, treated like any other: sources, practice, artifacts. Not a reading
list about a problem.

Three modes, rotating. Do not let *supporting* be the one that gets skipped — it is the
mode that produces evidence, and the one people quietly drop when a week gets busy.

## Navigating

The mechanics of visibility, sponsorship, credit, feedback, negotiation.

- Who in this org can say my name in a room I am not in? What do they know to say?
- What did I do this month that nobody senior watched me do?
- Where did I take on work that is necessary, invisible, and not mine?

## Structural

How pipeline and promotion systems actually work, with data. Reading research rather than
takes makes you the person in the room who can cite something.

- Kapor Center (Leaky Tech Pipeline, Tech Leavers) · Project Include · NCWIT.
- What does my own org's data say, and can I actually get it?

## Supporting

A concrete act, logged. Sponsorship you did is staff evidence.

- Review someone's promo packet or résumé.
- Run a mock interview.
- Answer a question in a community channel where the asker is earlier than you.
- Put a name forward for something, to someone who can act on it.

## Sources

Minda Harts, *The Memo* · Ruchika Tulshyan, *Inclusion on Purpose* · Kapor Center
research · *Blacks In Technology* podcast · Karla Monterroso · Erica Joy Baker.
