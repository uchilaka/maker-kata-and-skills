# Track — systems & architecture (Mon)

Distributed systems, data, failure modes. Study day: new input, no rep.

## Prompts

- What does this fail like? Not "does it fail" — what is the observable symptom first.
- Where is the state, and who can write it?
- What would this cost at 100× the volume, and which dimension breaks first?
- What did the author know that made this the obvious choice at the time?
- Which of today's claims could I falsify with an experiment this week?
